## The core problem: the headline object already exists
- If both the rate volatility and the vol-of-vol are scaled by the Lyashenko-Mercurio decay g(t), the "SABR-FMM" is EXACTLY constant-parameter SABR under the deterministic time change tau(t) = integral of g^2. The caplet formula is Hagan with T replaced by tau(T). One-line lemma.
- Willems (2020, "SABR smiles for RFR caplets", arXiv 2004.04501) already derives effective SABR parameters for compounded-in-arrears caplets.
- Taipe-Silvestre (2022, SSRN 4046344, "Tuning the FMM-SABR for RFR caplets") is a calibration study of exactly this model. Must be read before any proposal.
- Adachi, Fukasawa et al. (Sep 2025, arXiv 2509.25975, "Rough SABR Forward Market Model") give rigorous swaption implied-vol asymptotics in a (rough) SABR FMM.
- Piterbarg (2020, SSRN 3537925) covers the effect of fallbacks on caps, swaptions, in-arrears swaps and range accruals for LIBOR.
- Turfus & Romero-Bermudez (2023), Brace-Gellert-Schlogl (2024), Fontana et al. (2023) cover smile-consistent compounded-rate options by other routes.
- "Effective beta and rho" in RQ1 is a misreading: beta is a functional-form choice and rho is unaffected by a common scaling.
- Geography is not novelty. "No ZAR paper exists" does not constitute a gap.

## The data problem: two of four questions cannot be falsified
- No ZARONIA options market exists and no ZARONIA volatility quotes are published. SARB MPG published non-linear conventions (Nov 2024) but options "will in future be quoted on screens".
- The JIBAR smile itself is thin: five or six banks via brokers, ATM plus a few wing strikes, sparse swaption grid. Bloomberg VCUB ZAR is interpolated marks. Fitting 4-parameter SABR to ~3 reliable points per expiry.
- JIBAR cap/swaption vol surfaces are dealer-confidential; not obtainable without a bank sponsor. Without a sponsor this is a simulation thesis.
- Smile transport (RQ2) is unidentifiable: with no ZARONIA options, every transported smile is consistent with the data. Under the ISDA fallback a legacy JIBAR caplet post-cessation IS a compounded-ZARONIA caplet with a CAS-shifted strike; the vol relation is Piterbarg plus the time change: a corollary.
- Desks already handle this via vendor systems (Murex, Calypso, Numerix) shipping SOFR/SONIA caplet models: keep JIBAR surface, shift strike by CAS, scale vol, small convexity adjustment. Nobody in Johannesburg builds a SABR-FMM from scratch; the market is too small.

## What survives and how to make it doctoral
Genuinely open problems in the vicinity (theorist):
1. TENOR CONSISTENCY / CLOSURE. Compounded rates over nested windows satisfy (1+R_13 D_13) = (1+R_12 D_12)(1+R_23 D_23) identically; unlike LIBOR tenors there is no basis to hide behind. FMM models non-overlapping tenors and ignores this. SABR is not closed under compounding: which families of per-tenor SABR parameters are generated by a single daily-rate SV process? Nobody has done this.
2. RIGOROUS ASYMPTOTICS FOR TIME-INHOMOGENEOUS SABR (rate vol decays, vol-of-vol does not, the physically defensible choice): heat kernel on a time-dependent metric with error bounds. Hagan-style averaging is heuristic.
3. CONVEXITY between in-advance term rate and in-arrears compounded rate under stochastic vol AND stochastic basis: rho-dependent rigorous asymptotic, not Mercurio's lognormal adjustment.
4. IDENTIFIABILITY DECOMPOSITION of the smile map: provably identifiable part (fallback strike shift + time change, with bounds) vs non-identifiable part (basis-vol correlation). An impossibility theorem ("the compounded-rate smile is not identified from term-rate options plus linear instruments without assumption X") is doctoral. Validate on the USD LIBOR-to-SOFR natural experiment 2021-23 where both option markets traded, then apply to ZAR.
5. FALLBACK AS A STOPPING TIME: cessation-date uncertainty, pull-to-fallback of the basis spread as a bridge process, no-arbitrage constraints on basis-swap dynamics. Modest but publishable.

## Strongest plausible main theorem (theorist)
Let overnight forward rates follow a common-driver SABR-type stochastic-volatility process. Then (i) each compounded rate over window [T, T+Delta] admits an effective SABR parameterisation (alpha, beta, rho, nu)_eff given explicitly as functionals of Delta and g, with an O(Delta^2) error bound; (ii) across nested/overlapping windows these parameters satisfy explicit necessary-and-sufficient no-arbitrage constraints; (iii) the convexity adjustment between the forward-looking term rate and the compounded rate is rho-dependent in closed asymptotic form, isolating exactly which component of any JIBAR-to-ZARONIA smile map is identifiable from JIBAR options plus basis swaps and which is not.
