# Recommended PhD topic (synthesis of four council rounds, 2026-09-09)

## Title
Pricing and Hedging Through a Benchmark Transition Without an Options Market:
Identifiability, Smile Transport and Finite-Sample Model-Risk Certification for the JIBAR-to-ZARONIA Derivatives Book

## One-paragraph statement
On 31 December 2026 JIBAR ceases and every legacy JIBAR cap, floor and swaption in South Africa becomes, by ISDA fallback, an option on compounded-in-arrears ZARONIA plus a fixed credit adjustment spread. Unlike the USD LIBOR-to-SOFR transition, the target rate has no options market and none is expected before the transition completes, so banks must value and hedge a converted option book against a volatility smile that cannot be observed. The thesis answers two questions the market currently answers by convention. First, a mathematical one: exactly which part of the ZARONIA-linked smile is identified from the JIBAR option surface, basis swaps and the fallback spread, and which part is not. Second, a statistical one: for the part that is not identified, what finite-sample, distribution-free bound on the realised hedging error of a chosen valuation and hedging policy can be certified from data, and does it beat the block-bootstrap and traffic-light backtests that model validation uses today. The USD LIBOR-to-SOFR transition of 2021-2023, where both option markets eventually traded, is the natural experiment on which the theory is validated before it is applied to ZAR.

## Why this topic survives the council objections
- Not a relabelled SABR-FMM: the compounded-rate stochastic-vol model is a two-page lemma in chapter 2 (constant-parameter SABR under a deterministic time change; Willems 2020, Taipe-Silvestre 2022). The contribution is the identifiability theorem, not the model.
- Not unfalsifiable: the identifiability decomposition is validated on USD data where the answer became observable; the hedging-error certificate is computed on realised JIBAR and ZARONIA data, which exist.
- Not a coverage claim that is "almost free": conformal certificates are for a FIXED policy (no false policy-dependence claim), the coverage gap is stated in total variation / W1 on the scalar score law (not adapted Wasserstein), with an impossibility direction (no distribution-free certificate is non-vacuous beyond a drift threshold) as the fallback theorem.
- Not geography-as-novelty: the theorems are rate-agnostic (SOFR, SONIA, ESTR); ZARONIA is the case study and the unique dataset.
- Not a trivial superhedging number: the ambiguity set is the identified/non-identified decomposition itself, which is bounded and data-driven.
- Plays to the candidate: the statistical strand (finite-sample certification, dependent data, minimax impossibility) is Mathematical Statistics; the finance strand is learnable in year one with local supervision.

## Research questions
1. IDENTIFIABILITY (mathematical finance). Under a common-driver stochastic-volatility model for overnight rates, decompose the map from (term-rate smile, basis swaps, fallback spread) to the compounded-rate smile into an identified component (fallback strike shift + deterministic time change + in-advance/in-arrears convexity, with explicit error bounds) and a non-identified component (basis-volatility correlation and higher-order terms). Prove an impossibility theorem: the compounded-rate smile is not identified from term-rate options plus linear instruments without an assumption on X, and characterise the set of consistent smiles.
2. TENOR CONSISTENCY (mathematical finance, second theorem). Compounded rates over nested windows satisfy an exact multiplicative identity with no basis to hide behind. Which families of per-tenor SABR parameters are generated by a single daily-rate stochastic-volatility process, and what no-arbitrage constraints does this impose on the transported smile?
3. CERTIFICATION (mathematical statistics). For a fixed valuation-and-hedging policy applied to the converted book (the market's convention, or the model of RQ1), construct a split-conformal certificate on realised hedging P&L under beta-mixing with an explicit coverage gap in total variation on the score law; prove when it out-guarantees stationary block bootstrap and historical-simulation quantiles; prove the impossibility direction under drift beyond a threshold. Frame as a finite-sample challenger to FRTB P&L-attribution tests and SR 11-7 model validation.
4. APPLICATION (empirical, one chapter). Validate RQ1-RQ3 on USD LIBOR caps vs SOFR caps 2021-2023; apply to the ZAR book through the completed 2026-2027 transition; quantify the model-risk reserve implied by the non-identified component and compare to the "spread of five models" practice and the market's CAS-shift-plus-scaled-vol convention.

## Plausible main theorems
- T1 (identifiability): explicit decomposition with O(Delta^2) error on the identified part and a characterisation of the consistent set for the non-identified part; sharpness by a two-model counterexample matching all JIBAR options and basis swaps with different compounded-rate smiles.
- T2 (certification): for stationary beta-mixing hedging P&L, calibration on n blocks of length b disjoint from the fitting sample, the weighted split-conformal quantile satisfies coverage >= 1 - alpha - d_TV(score laws) - C sqrt(b log(1/delta)/n) - n beta(b), with a matching lower bound showing the TV term cannot be removed by any distribution-free method.
