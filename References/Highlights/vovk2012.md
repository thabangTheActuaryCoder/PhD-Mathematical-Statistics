## Notes for writers
- Cite `\citep[Prop.~2b]{vovk2012}` for the exact conditional-coverage law and `\citep[Prop.~2a]{vovk2012}` for the Hoeffding corollary. Do not attribute the phrase "Beta(k, n+1−k) law" to Vovk 2012; write "the Beta form of Vovk's Proposition 2b" and prove the equivalence (ch2 Theorem thm:prelim-beta already does).
- Vovk's setting is i.i.d. calibration + i.i.d. test; the thesis's extension to β-mixing calibration is what is new (see LESSONS, scout-literature 2026-09-09).
