**What the thesis needs from it.** The definition of the moving block bootstrap (ch3 §"Block bootstraps", ch6 empirical comparison) and the fact that Künsch proves variance/moment consistency for the arithmetic mean, not a finite-sample coverage statement (supports the LESSON "do not call the bootstrap comparison a theorem").

**Notes for writers.** Künsch's mixing coefficient is strong (α-) mixing, not β-mixing. His block count assumes n = kl exactly; the thesis's ⌈N/ℓ⌉ with truncation is the standard later convention (Politis–Romano) and should not be attributed to Künsch verbatim.
