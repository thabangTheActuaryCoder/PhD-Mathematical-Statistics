**What the thesis needs from it.** Theorem `thm:prelim-dkw` (ch2, stated and cited, not proved) and the "DKW form as a corollary" paragraph in ch3 (Thm 6(ii)). The thesis already says the constant 2 is Massart's; DKW's own statement has an unspecified constant `c`. DKW's Lemma 2 is stated for the **uniform d.f. and the one-sided deviation** `sup[S_n(x) − x]` first (eq. 2.5), two-sided (eq. 2.6) second.
