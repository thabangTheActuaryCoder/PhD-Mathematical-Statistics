## Notes for writers
- Cite the count as `\citep[Cor.~2.7 and Lemma~4.1]{yu1994}`; Lemma 4.1 is Corollary 2.7 applied to the block sequence.
- Yu's sequence is strictly stationary and β_k is the stationary coefficient; the thesis's non-stationary sup definition is a strengthening the thesis must state explicitly (ch2).
- Yu's blocks are separated by a discarded block of the same length (gap = block length), matching Assumption mix.
