**Notes for writers.** Cite this paper for "no distribution-free rate under ergodicity alone", not for "β(b) is not estimable". The non-estimability claim needs its own source or a `\gap{Citation needed}`; a lead to verify (not verified here) is McDonald, Shalizi and Schervish, "Estimating beta-mixing coefficients via histograms", *Electron. J. Statist.* 9 (2015), which in fact proposes an estimator under assumptions — so the thesis sentence should be softened to "not estimable without assumptions on the process".
