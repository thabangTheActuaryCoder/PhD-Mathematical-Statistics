**What the thesis needs from it.** (i) The marginal-vs-calibration-conditional distinction behind GROUND-TRUTH corrections 10–11 (Thm 6(ii), Thm 9); (ii) conditional coverage reformulated as coverage over a class of covariate shifts, the covariate-shift analogue of the thesis's drift term d_K(P,Q); (iii) related work in ch3/ch4.

**Notes for writers.** All results assume i.i.d. (or exchangeable) calibration data; there is no dependence or drift in this paper. Cite it for the conditional/marginal taxonomy and the shift-class view, not for anything about mixing.
