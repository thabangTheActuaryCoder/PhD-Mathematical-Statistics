**Notes for writers.** (i) The expansion parameter is ε with ε = 1 restored ("Apart from setting ε to 1 to recover the original units", p. 102); "through O(ε²)" is the printed error order, consistent with the LESSON that neglected terms are relative O(ε⁴) only for the β ∈ {0,1} special cases ("Both these models are simple enough that the expansion can be continued through O(ε⁴)", p. 102). (ii) Cross-reference misprints in the original: "Appendix B" for the special cases (should be A.5); "Repeating the analysis in Appendix B through equation (A.10a), (A.10b)" on p. 103 (should be Appendix A). (iii) Backbone discussion is §3, p. 90.
