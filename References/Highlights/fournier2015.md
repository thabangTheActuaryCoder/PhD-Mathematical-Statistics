# fournier2015 — Fournier & Guillin (2015), empirical Wasserstein rates

**Record.** Fournier, N., Guillin, A. (2015). On the rate of convergence in Wasserstein distance of the empirical measure. *Probability Theory and Related Fields* 162(3–4), 707–738. DOI 10.1007/s00440-014-0583-7 (published online 18 Oct 2014). Bib entry verified via Crossref: correct.

**File.** `References/PDF/fournier2015.pdf` = arXiv:1312.2128v1 (7 Dec 2013, the only arXiv version, 25 pp), licence arXiv non-exclusive. Journal version is paywalled (Springer); **theorem numbers and pages below are the arXiv v1 numbering**; journal numbering unverified. Highlighted copy: `fournier2015.highlighted.pdf`.

**What the thesis needs from it.** Prop 7 / Thm 9(v) (dependent empirical W₁, ch3–ch4): the i.i.d. rate in d = 1 and the fact that the same rate holds for ρ-mixing sequences with summable coefficients. GROUND-TRUTH now assigns the β-mixing case to Dedecker–Merlevède (2017); Fournier–Guillin is the ρ-mixing alternative and the source of the moment-condition form of the rate.
