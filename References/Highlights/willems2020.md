## Notes for writers
- **Attribution:** ground-truth Lemma 1 (same decay on rate and volatility ⇒ exact time change to constant-parameter SABR at clock τ(T+Δ)) is NOT Willems's model. Willems decays only the rate diffusion and prices with HLW effective parameters (Theorems 4.1–4.2). Cite willems2020 for Prop 2 (non-decaying vol-of-vol, effective parameters) and for the linear-decay/mean-reversion link (App. A); cite the exact-time-change lemma to lyashenko2019 or prove it (ch2 does prove it).
- Willems works in Black (lognormal) implied vol with β ∈ [0,1]; the thesis uses normal vol with β = 0. State the conversion once.
