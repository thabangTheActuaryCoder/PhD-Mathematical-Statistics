Intended use (ch6 empirical chapter, acceptance criteria stated in advance): the conditional-coverage test — an interval forecast is efficient if the hit sequence I_t = 1{y_t ∈ interval} is i.i.d. Bernoulli(1 − α); tests of unconditional coverage (LR_uc), independence (LR_ind, first-order Markov) and conditional coverage (LR_cc = LR_uc + LR_ind ~ χ²(2)). Standard section locators are §2 (definition of conditional efficiency) and §3 (the LR tests); confirm on the copy before writing `\citep[§3]{christoffersen1998}`. Note for ch6: the LR_ind test presumes a Markov alternative and is asymptotic; it is a diagnostic, not a certificate, and must not be presented as a finite-sample guarantee.
