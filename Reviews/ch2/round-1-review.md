## 4. Missing for a strong doctoral chapter

The chapter is complete as a toolbox and its proofs hold; what a doctoral examiner will miss is not more theorems but the two anchors that make the preliminaries visibly the thesis's own. First, the effective-parameter proposition (prop:prelim-nueff) is the one piece of finance mathematics in the chapter that is not lifted from a textbook, and it is currently unmoored: it must be tied to the published closed forms (Willems 2020, Thm 4.2 at q = 1; Hagan et al. 2002, App. B) by an explicit comparison, even a one-line "the weights agree/disagree with ...", or it reads as a private computation. Second, the statistics half should state each standard tool in exactly the generality Chapter 3 needs (Beta law with atoms, AR(1) bound without a side condition, non-stationary beta with the stationary Yu definition named), so that Chapter 3 can consist of the certificate alone; at present Chapter 3 re-proves five of these lemmas because Chapter 2 stopped one step short. A short basis-dynamics definition (item 31) and a chapter-notes paragraph placing the blocked route against Barber and Pananjady (2026) (item 20) complete the picture. Nothing else should be added; the chapter is over budget.
